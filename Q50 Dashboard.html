Compare two PCFs — what changed

Net trade strips out creations and redemptions: the "From" holding is first scaled by the change in units in issue (in-kind baskets move every name pro rata), and only the difference is treated as a buy or sell. Corporate actions such as splits or bonus issues will show as trades. Trade value uses the "To" price (the "From" price for exits). Turnover = smaller of buys and sells ÷ average NAV.
